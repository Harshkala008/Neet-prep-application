======================================================================
1. MAIN PURPOSE
======================================================================

NEET Master Tracker is a NEET UG 2026 syllabus and preparation-progress
tracking application. It is primarily a checklist and analytics app for
tracking chapters, subtopics, study resources, tests, mistakes, revision
confidence, and study time.

The app helps a student:

- Track the entire NEET UG 2026 syllabus.
- Break subjects into units and smaller topics.
- Mark study resources as completed.
- Track lectures, modules, DPPs, NCERT reading, PYQs, and revision.
- Record confidence for every topic.
- Set revision dates.
- Store personal notes.
- Log mistakes from tests or practice.
- Record mock-test scores.
- Track study time using a timer.
- View study streaks and recent activity.
- Export/import progress data.
- Optionally sign in to synchronize progress across devices.

Initial empty tracker state:

- 50 total units
- 342 trackable topics/items
- 0 completed
- 0 in progress
- 342 not started
- 0 needing revision

21. HOW PROGRESS LIKELY WORKS
======================================================================

NOT STARTED

No tracked resource is checked.

IN PROGRESS

At least one resource is checked, but the topic is not marked Done.

COMPLETED

The Done status is checked.

NEEDS REVISION

The next revision date has passed, or the topic has a low-confidence or
revision-required state.

RESOURCE COMPLETION

Completed applicable topics / applicable topics

N/A topics are removed from the denominator.

OVERALL COMPLETION

Overall completion appears to be calculated from tracked topic/item
status, rather than only from the number of chapters.

This is why the app reports:

342 trackable items

instead of only reporting 50 units.

26. RECOMMENDED AUTOMATIC PLANNING ALGORITHM
======================================================================

A better planner can calculate topic priority using:

priority =
  examImportance
  x weaknessScore
  x revisionUrgency
  x resourcePendingScore
  x difficulty

Example:

weaknessScore:
1 - confidence / 5

revisionUrgency:
Higher when nextRevisionDate is overdue.

resourcePendingScore:
Number of incomplete resources / applicable resources

difficulty:
Easy = 1
Medium = 1.5
Hard = 2

The planner should distribute topics across available daily hours.

It should ensure:

- No subject is ignored.
- Biology receives appropriate time because of its question weight.
- Revision is mixed with new learning.
- Tests are inserted periodically.
- Hard topics receive longer blocks.
- Daily workload is realistic.

29. FINAL ASSESSMENT
======================================================================

The current application has a strong foundation for a NEET tracker. Its
main strengths are its detailed syllabus hierarchy, resource-level
checklist, confidence ratings, mistake logging, test tracking, study
timer, and export functionality.

Its largest limitations are:

- Basic planner
- Limited test analytics
- No built-in educational resources
- No reminders
- Limited automation

To create a better version, preserve the hierarchical tracker and add:

1. Personalized onboarding.
2. A smart daily planner.
3. Automatic spaced revision.
4. Detailed test analytics.
5. Integrated mistake-to-topic analysis.
6. Resource links and content organization.
7. Notifications and reminders.
8. Mobile-first PWA behavior.
9. Offline support.
10. Stronger visual progress and performance insights.

The live app is useful as a checklist and progress database. A stronger
product should actively guide the student on:

- What to study next
- Why it should be studied
- How long it should take
- Whether the student is improving
